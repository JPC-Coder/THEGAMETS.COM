The GAMETS Collectible Cards are an original series of non-sports trading cards created by artist Juan Pablo Campos and JPC Creative Studios. Each card introduces a unique GAMET—a strange, colorful character brought to life by mysterious Gamet Goo. Every card features bold cartoon artwork, vivid colors, dramatic backgrounds, and an urban graffiti-inspired style. The characters have their own names, numbers, personalities, powers, weaknesses, elements, alliances, enemies, favorite foods, hobbies, attack points, and defense points. The collection includes regular cards, sketch cards, gold cards, rare editions, and limited collectible designs. Some GAMETS are funny and friendly, while others are wild, mysterious, powerful, or mischievous. Together, they create a growing universe filled with imagination, adventure, humor, and original stories. Collectors can search for their favorite characters, complete each numbered series, trade cards with others, and discover rare GAMETS that may become valuable pieces of the collection. Collect them. -The Rise of the Gamets-

On the distant Planet Gamet, a world of shining metal cities and endless invention, robots created the greatest machine the universe had ever seen. It was called the Reproducer, a powerful device that could create anything in seconds—food, tools, homes, even entire machines. The robots built it with one purpose: to make the universe a better place. For a time, it did. Worlds once filled with hunger and need were transformed by the wonders of Gamet technology.

But one terrible day, the Reproducer malfunctioned. The machine, which had always worked at impossible speed, overloaded and exploded with unstoppable force. Its chambers burst apart, sending glowing chemicals and streams of green slime across the stars. The strange substance rained down on Mars, the Moon, and Earth, soaking into junkyards, garbage dumps, abandoned cars, broken furniture, and piles of old trash. Everywhere it landed, something impossible began to happen.

The green goo gave life to what had once been useless. Rusted cars shuddered awake. Broken chairs crawled on twisted legs. Mountains of scrap metal fused together and formed living creatures with minds of their own. These beings became known as the Gamets.

Some were kind and brave, using their strange new powers to protect life. Others were cruel, angry, and hungry for control. The good Gamets saw Earth as a world worth saving. The evil Gamets saw it as a planet worth conquering.

Soon, the race began to save Planet Earth from total destruction. Teams were formed across the globe, and the battle lines were drawn: good versus evil, Gamet against Gamet, with humans caught in the middle. Some humans joined forces with the good Gamets, believing that together they could defend Earth and restore balance. Others betrayed their own world and sided with the evil Gamets, hoping to share in their power and rule over what remained. From Mars and the Moon, even more Gamets arrived. Some came as warriors determined to protect Earth at all costs. Others came as invaders, ready to claim the planet for themselves.

Every day, new Gamets rose from toxic waste dumps, junkyards, and garbage sites around the world, making the struggle even more dangerous. What began as a dream to improve the universe had become a constant battle for survival. Now Earth stands on the edge of destruction, and only the strongest alliances between humans and good Gamets can stop the evil forces before it is too late.

Story by Juan Pablo Campos
